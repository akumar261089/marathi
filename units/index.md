# 📚 COMPLETE MARATHI COURSE INDEX

### Final Outcome

After completing **Units 1–20 + G1–G8**, a Hindi speaker should be able to:

- Speak comfortably in Maharashtra
- Understand everyday conversations
- Read newspapers and signs
- Write messages, emails, and short essays
- Understand most Marathi grammar encountered in daily life
- Reach approximately **B2 conversational Marathi**.
